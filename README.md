# Jeremy Chu

Hi, I’m Jeremy.

I build software and data systems for operating businesses. Most of my work has
been in solar and energy, distribution, logistics, manufacturing, and finance.

My background is a mix of operating and technical roles. I started in finance,
co-founded a manufacturing and logistics business, led technology for solar
companies, and continued working as an architect and engineer.

I tend to work on systems behind the scenes: internal applications, APIs,
enterprise integrations, data pipelines, financial workflows, and tools used
by operations and engineering teams. I enjoy understanding how a business
works and then building something practical around it.

## Background

Through Flicker Ventures, I have worked with Soligent for several years,
including as Head of Technology and later as a Group Data Engineer. The work
has covered enterprise applications, data and reporting, NetSuite, inventory,
finance, logistics, tax, e-commerce, and customer integrations.

Earlier in my career, I was an operating partner and technology lead for a
residential solar installer, a co-founder of a contract manufacturing and
global logistics company, and the founding technology lead for an investment
firm.

I still work directly in the code, while also spending time on architecture,
product decisions, operations, and the business context around a system.
