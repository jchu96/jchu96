# Jeremy Chu

**Operator-architect building reliable systems for complex businesses.**

I work where software, data, operations, and applied AI meet. My career has
moved from finance and trading to founding and operating businesses, leading
technology teams, designing enterprise platforms, and building production
systems hands-on.

The common thread is operational complexity. I am usually most useful when the
real problem crosses departments and systems: finance does not reconcile with
operations, important data is trapped across platforms, a workflow depends on
spreadsheets and institutional memory, or a promising AI prototype needs the
controls required for real use.

I like understanding how the business actually works, turning that understanding
into a clear model, and staying close enough to implementation to make sure the
system survives contact with reality.

## What I do

### Turn operations into systems

I translate fragmented processes into software that people can actually adopt.
That has included platforms for investment operations, global logistics,
contract manufacturing, residential solar, distribution, inventory, finance,
and permit-design workflows.

### Architect across boundaries

My work rarely fits inside one application. I design the contracts and
workflows connecting ERP, WMS, TMS, CRM, tax, finance, e-commerce, analytics,
field operations, and customer-facing products. I care about idempotency,
traceability, data quality, permissions, failure recovery, and the human
handoffs between systems.

### Build the implementation

I remain a hands-on engineer. Depending on the problem, that can mean a
full-stack application, an API or webhook boundary, a data pipeline, an
interactive domain model, a native desktop integration, an operational
runbook, or the test and release infrastructure around all of it.

### Apply AI with operational discipline

I use AI as an augmentation layer for research, analysis, software delivery,
and decision support. I am particularly interested in agentic systems, context
engineering, and human-in-the-loop workflows that preserve provenance, expose
uncertainty, respect privacy, and fail safely.

## Selected public work

### [Frame of Mind](https://github.com/jchu96/frame-of-mind)

Video in, understanding out. A local-first, recipe-driven workflow that combines
meeting context with screen recordings and Gemini video understanding to
produce structured, reviewable analysis. It supports decisions, requirements,
action items, repository plans, and issue reviews while keeping provenance,
privacy boundaries, and human review explicit.

### [WhatsApp Echo](https://github.com/jchu96/whatsapp-echo)

An earlier privacy-oriented voice-note transcription experiment that turns
audio into raw transcripts and optional AI-assisted summaries through email and
an API. It explores low-friction interfaces, ephemeral media processing, user
controls, and practical serverless deployment.
