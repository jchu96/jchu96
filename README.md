# Jeremy Chu

I'm an architect and full-stack engineer specializing in enterprise systems across a range of industries — from financial services and supply-chain logistics to solar and construction. A former finance executive turned entrepreneur, I founded, scaled, and exited a logistics venture whose platforms managed over 15,000 ocean freight containers annually, then launched a residential solar installation company and served as Head of Technology for the largest US solar distributor through a private equity acquisition. That work gave me a comprehensive understanding of the U.S. residential solar market — sales, engineering, permitting, and distribution — that I still build on today.

My current focus is applied AI. I'm the lead engineer on a design platform for the solar construction industry that turns a rough solar design into permit-ready engineering — drawings, wire sizing, code compliance — in minutes instead of hours. I built it end to end, with the AI grounded in verified sources because my users are engineers who stop trusting a tool the first time it's wrong. Beyond the design tool, I integrate AI across the company's operational platform — the ERPs, CRMs, and industry systems where the business actually runs.

The pattern across my career is the same: embed inside a complex operation, learn the business as well as the people running it, and ship the system they adopt. I'm most interested now in the deployment problem rather than the demo problem — what it takes to make AI genuinely trustworthy inside businesses that can't afford for it to be confidently wrong.
