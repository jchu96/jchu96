builder.

## What I’ve built

### Solar design and engineering software

I currently lead the development of a solar design platform that connects a
browser-based electrical design tool with a native AutoCAD engine. The system
handles electrical modeling, engineering checks, wire schedules, bills of
materials, collaborative review, and production drawings.

The interesting part is not any one screen or calculation. It is keeping the
design, engineering data, material requirements, and final drawing consistent
as several people and applications work on the same job.

### Enterprise systems and integrations

At Soligent, my work has covered NetSuite, inventory, warehouse and
transportation systems, e-commerce, sales tax, finance, customer APIs, data
warehouses, and reporting.

Some projects are large platform efforts. Others are small but important:
making sure a financial correction reaches every downstream system, preventing
bad data from moving through a webhook, or giving a customer API the fields and
documentation people actually need.

### Data and analytics

I have designed data warehouses, pipelines, and reporting systems across Azure
and AWS using SQL Server, Azure SQL, Databricks, PySpark, Fivetran, dbt,
Redshift, Power BI, Tableau, and QuickSight.

The goal has usually been practical: help people understand margins, inventory,
cash flow, supply-chain performance, and what needs attention next.

### Applied AI

More recently, I have been working deeply with AI coding agents, multimodal
analysis, context and memory systems, and human review workflows.

I am most interested in AI that helps people do difficult work better—not AI
added to a product for the sake of having an AI feature. I care about evidence,
privacy, uncertainty, and making it clear when a person still needs to make the
decision.
